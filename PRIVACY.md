# Privacy Policy

**App:** Yumibun (iOS and Android)
**Developer:** Toepper.Rocks
**Last updated:** July 29, 2026

Yumibun is an ambient‑sound mixer for focus, relaxation, and sleep. It is
designed to work entirely on your device. **We do not collect, store, or share
any personal information, and the app contains no accounts, analytics,
advertising, or third‑party tracking.**

If you only remember one thing: nothing you do in Yumibun leaves your device
unless *you* explicitly choose to share it.

## Information we collect

**None.** Yumibun does not collect personal data. There is no sign‑up, no login,
and no user profile. We have no servers that receive your data.

## Data stored on your device

To make the app work and remember your choices, the following is saved **locally
on your device only** (in the system's standard app storage — `UserDefaults` on
iOS, `SharedPreferences` on Android):

- Your favorite sounds
- Saved mixes ("presets")
- Volume levels and the currently selected sounds
- App settings (appearance/theme, accent color, home‑screen style, language,
  timer/alarm volume, and vibration preference)

This information never leaves your device. It is removed when you uninstall the
app.

## No network use

Yumibun ships all of its sounds and images inside the app. On Android the app
does not request the `INTERNET` permission, and on both platforms it makes no
network requests of its own — there is no telemetry, crash reporting, or
background communication.

## Sharing a mix

Yumibun lets you share a mix if you choose to. When you tap **Share**, the app
builds a link that encodes only the technical makeup of the mix — the sound
identifiers and their volume levels (for example
`https://moodist.toepper.rocks/?share=...`). The link contains **no personal
information**.

The link is handed to your operating system's standard share sheet, and *you*
decide whether and where to send it (Messages, email, etc.). Yumibun does not
transmit it anywhere on its own. Once shared, the content is handled by whatever
app or service you send it to, under that service's own privacy policy.

Opening a shared link simply loads that combination of sounds into the app.

## Links to external sites

The Settings screen contains links (project website, source code, licenses, and
artwork/sound attribution). Opening one leaves Yumibun and opens your browser or
the relevant app. Those third‑party sites have their own privacy policies, which
we do not control.

## Permissions

Yumibun requests only the permissions it needs to play audio; none are used to
collect information about you:

- **Notifications** — to show playback controls (play/pause) and timer/alarm
  notifications.
- **Background playback / foreground service** (Android) — to keep sounds
  playing while the app is in the background or the screen is off.
- **Keep‑awake (wake lock)** (Android) — to keep audio playing reliably during a
  session.
- **Vibration** (Android) — for the optional timer/alarm vibration.

## Language

You can run Yumibun in a language different from your device language. This
preference is applied by the operating system's per‑app language feature and is
stored on your device; it is not shared with us.

## Third‑party content

The bundled sounds and category images come from third‑party sources (such as
Pixabay and Wikimedia Commons) under their respective licenses. These assets are
included in the app; using them involves no data collection. See `ATTRIBUTION.md`
and the in‑app License section for details.

## Children's privacy

Yumibun does not collect any data from anyone, including children. The app
contains no accounts, ads, or tracking.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published here with a new
"Last updated" date. Material changes will also be reflected in the app or its
store listing.
